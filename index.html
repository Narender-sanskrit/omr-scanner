OMR Scanner

Put it on your home screen

Install once and it behaves like any other app: tap the icon, it opens full screen, no address bar, and it works with no internet.

Button not showing? Do it by hand
1Android, Chrome: tap the ⋮ menu (top right) → Install app or Add to Home screen → Install.
2iPhone, Safari: tap the Share icon (square with an arrow) → scroll down → Add to Home Screen → Add.
3Open the github.io link, not a file saved on the phone — the camera only works on a secure (https) page.

Answer key

Fill it once. Every sheet is checked against it.

Letters A–D (up to your template's option count), or digits 1,2,3,4. Use - to leave a question out of scoring.

Or tap the grid

Students (optional)

Roll number is read from the bubbles. Name and class are looked up here.

Format: roll,name,class — one student per line. Without it you still get roll numbers and scores.

Scoring

Lower = catches faint pencil, but may read smudges as answers.

If the second-darkest circle is this close to the darkest, the question is flagged for you instead of being guessed.

How it works

1 Print the sheet from the Sheet template tab (or use your own).
2 Fill the key above, load your roster, then go to Scan.
3 Photograph each sheet — all four black corner squares must be visible.
4 Review anything the app flagged, then export CSV / JSON.

Everything runs inside this browser tab. No uploads, no accounts, no per-scan cost. Photos are discarded as soon as a sheet is read — only the row of results is kept.

Camera
